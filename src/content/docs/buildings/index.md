Every building can be upgraded from level 1 up to **level 30**. Costs and build times grow with each level, so the later levels are a long-term investment. Click a building to see its cost, build time and effect at every level.

:::note
A building can never be upgraded past your **Stronghold** level, and many buildings have other requirements. Each building page lists them.
:::

## Core

| Building | Limit | What it does |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Stronghold](stronghold/) | 1 | The heart of your kingdom. Upgrade it to unlock buildings and raise your power. |
| [Academy](academy/) | 1 | Unlocks the Research tree, which is required to study all technology branches. |

## Economy

Production buildings fill up over time. Tap them to **collect**. Each one stores about **3 hours** of production, so collect regularly or the production is wasted.

| Building | Limit | Produces | Per level, per hour |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| [Farm](farm/) | 2 | Food | 365 |
| [Sawmill](sawmill/) | 2 | Wood | 550 |
| [Quarry](quarry/) | 2 | Stone | 260 |
| [Treasury](treasury/) | 2 | Gold | 200 |

Production and storage both scale with the building's level (level 10 is ten times level 1), and research can boost them further.

| Building | Limit | What it does |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Warehouse](warehouse/) | 1 | Protects your resources from raiders. Resources stored below the protection threshold cannot be looted. The protection grows with every level up to 30. |

## Military

| Building | Limit | What it does |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Barracks](barracks/) | 1 | Trains infantry. Higher levels unlock higher infantry tiers. |
| [Archery Range](archery-range/) | 1 | Trains archers. Higher levels unlock higher archer tiers. |
| [Stable](stable/) | 1 | Trains cavalry. Higher levels unlock higher cavalry tiers. |
| [Siege Workshop](siege-workshop/) | 1 | Builds siege engines. Higher levels unlock higher siege tiers. |
| [Guardhouse](guardhouse/) | 1 | Marks your kingdom's military readiness. It must stay one level behind the Stronghold for the Stronghold to keep upgrading, and it gates the Barracks. |
| [Drill Grounds](drill-grounds/) | 1 | Trains your armies to march and raid. Each level raises your march capacity by **200 troops**. |
| [War Hall](war-hall/) | 1 | Musters your guild's rallies. Each level raises the total troops a rally you start can carry by **200**. |
| [Embassy](embassy/) | 1 | Hosts allied reinforcements. Each level raises the total troops your castle can hold as reinforcements by **200**. |
| [Lighthouse](lighthouse/) | 1 | Required to send scouts. Each level sharpens what a scout report reveals: which troop tiers you can identify, how exact the troop counts are and, at high levels, the reinforcements stationed in the castle. |
| [Hospital](hospital/) | 1 | Holds wounded troops and heals them. Each level raises how many wounded it can hold by **200** and makes healing **2% faster**. Troops lost in battle are wounded first and only die if the Hospital is full. |

### Training speed

Every level of a training building (Barracks, Archery Range, Stable and Siege Workshop) makes its own troop type train **1% faster**, up to **30% at level 30**. This stacks with research.

See [Casualties and the Hospital](../combat/casualties-and-hospital/) and [Troops and stats](../combat/troops/) for how these buildings affect fighting.
