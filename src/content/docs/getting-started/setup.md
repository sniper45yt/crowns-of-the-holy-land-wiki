This page walks you through getting the game onto your device, creating an account and what to do the first time you open it.

:::note
The game is still in testing, so it is not in an app store yet. For now it runs on **Android**, and the install link is shared in the community Discord.
:::

## 1. Install the game

### On an Android phone or tablet

1. Join the game's Discord and find the post with the current **build link**.
2. Open that link on your Android device and download the build.
3. Open the downloaded file to install it. Android may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager. Allow it for this install.
4. When it finishes, open **Crowns of the Holy Land** from your app list.

New test builds are posted on Discord as the game is updated. Install the newest one when you see it.

### Without an Android device

If you do not have an Android phone, you can play on a PC with an Android emulator. **MuMu Player** is the one we recommend.

1. Install MuMu Player on your PC.
2. Open the build link from Discord inside MuMu Player's browser and download the build there, or drag the downloaded file onto the MuMu Player window.
3. Install it and open **Crowns of the Holy Land** from MuMu Player's app list.

## 2. Create your account

1. On the first screen, choose to create a new account.
2. Enter your **email address** and pick a **password**.
3. Follow any on-screen steps to finish, and you are in.

Next time you open the game, sign in with the same email and password to get back to your kingdom. Keep these details safe: your account is your kingdom.

## 3. Your first launch

When you first get into the game you will find a small stronghold waiting for you. A good way to start:

- **Look around your kingdom.** Tap a building to see what it does and what it needs. See [Buildings](../../buildings/).
- **Start building and upgrading.** Every building shows its cost before you begin. Timed actions keep running while you are away.
- **Collect your resources.** Production buildings fill up over time. Tap them to collect, and spend what you collect. See [Resources](../../game-systems/resources/).
- **Protect what you have.** Other players can attack you, so look at how shields and the Warehouse keep your stockpile safe. See [Attacking players](../../combat/attacking-players/).
- **Explore the map when you are ready.** See [Map and marching](../../game-systems/map-and-marching/) for how armies move around the world.

## Need help?

If the build link does not work, the install fails or you cannot sign in, ask in the community Discord. Tell us what device you are using and what you see on screen.
