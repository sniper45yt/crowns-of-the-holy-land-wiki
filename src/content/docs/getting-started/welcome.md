Crowns of the Holy Land is a multiplayer kingdom-building strategy game. You start with a small stronghold, grow your economy, train armies and compete with other players on a shared world map.

This wiki explains how the game's systems work. It is written by the developers and kept up to date as the game changes.

:::note
The game is still in testing, so numbers and mechanics can change between versions. Pages are updated as things settle.
:::
