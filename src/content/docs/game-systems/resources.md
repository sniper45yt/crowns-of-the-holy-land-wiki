title: Resources

---

Your kingdom runs on four resources, plus one premium currency.

| Resource | Produced by | Base output per building level, per hour |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Gold | Treasury | 200 |
| Food | Farm | 365 |
| Stone | Quarry | 260 |
| Wood | Sawmill | 550 |
| Silver | Not produced over time | - |

- Resources are spent on **constructing and upgrading buildings**, **training troops**, and other costs. Every building shows what it needs before you start.
- Production buildings store about **3 hours** of output. Tap a building to collect it.
- You can have **two** of each production building.
- Research can raise production and storage.

## Silver

Silver is a premium currency earned through gameplay. It is not produced over time. It is spent to instantly complete or speed up timed actions.

## Protecting your resources

Other players can steal resources when they win an attack against you. Stored resources **below your Warehouse's protection threshold cannot be looted**, so upgrade your Warehouse as your stockpile grows. See [Attacking players](../../combat/attacking-players/).
