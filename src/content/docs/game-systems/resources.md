title: Resources
description: The resources in Crowns of the Holy Land and how you get them.
---

Your kingdom runs on four resources, plus one premium currency.

| Resource | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Gold | _To be documented_ |
| Food | _To be documented_ |
| Stone | _To be documented_ |
| Wood | _To be documented_ |
| Silver | Premium currency earned through gameplay. Spent to instantly complete or speed up timed actions. It is not produced over time. |

Gold, food, stone and wood are produced by your buildings and collected from them.
