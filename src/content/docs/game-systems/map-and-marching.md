---
title: Map and marching
description: How the world map works and how armies travel.
---

The world is a grid of tiles shared by every player. Your kingdom sits on one tile, and you can send armies to other tiles to gather resources, fight non-player camps or attack other players.

Armies take time to travel, based on the distance to the destination.

Map size, march speed, tile types and camps will be documented here.
