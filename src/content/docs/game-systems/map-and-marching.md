---
title: Map and marching
description: How the world map works and how armies travel.
---

The world is a grid of tiles shared by every player. Your kingdom sits on one tile. From the map you can send armies to other tiles to gather resources, raid NPC camps or attack other players.

## Marches

- Each army you send out is a **march** and uses a **march slot**.
- The size of a march is limited by your **Drill Grounds**: 200 troops per level.
- Troops that are out on a march are not in your garrison, so they do not defend your castle.

## Travel time

Armies take **7.5 seconds per tile** of straight-line distance, with a minimum of 10 seconds. The type of march makes no difference. The return trip takes the same time as the way out.

| Movement | Speed |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary march | 7.5 s per tile |
| Rally | 15 s per tile (half speed) |
| Scout | 2.5 s per tile (three times faster) |

## What you can do on the map

- **Raid NPC camps** for loot and Lord XP. See [NPC camps](../../combat/npc-camps/).
- **Attack other players** to steal resources. See [Attacking players](../../combat/attacking-players/).
- **Reinforce guildmates** or start a **rally**. See [Rallies and reinforcements](../../combat/rallies-and-reinforcements/).
- **Scout** a castle first to see what it holds. This needs a Lighthouse.
