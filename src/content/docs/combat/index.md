## Quick tips

- **Bigger wins big.** Damage scales with the troops you have left, so a small lead snowballs. Avoid fights where you are outnumbered.
- **Know the cycle.** Infantry beat cavalry, cavalry beat archers, archers beat siege, siege beat infantry. It matters most when the armies are made of one or two types.
- **Build a Hospital.** Without one, every lost troop is dead.
- **Defending is cheaper than attacking.** Defenders' losses become wounded, but attackers only get 20% of theirs back.
- **Upgrade your Warehouse.** Resources under its threshold cannot be looted.
- **Use the walkthroughs** to estimate a fight before you start it.
