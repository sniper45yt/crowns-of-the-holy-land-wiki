There are four troop types (infantry, cavalry, archers and siege) and ten tiers. Higher tiers are tougher and hit harder, but cost more to train.

HP / ATK / DEF per troop, and the training cost (sum of all resources, the yardstick the balance was tuned against).

Power per troop is the same for every type at a tier: T1=5, T2=8, T3=12, T4=17, T5=23, T6=30, T7=38, T8=47, T9=58, T10=70 (it feeds no fight).

Roles by design: **infantry = meat shield** (lowest ATK, highest DEF and HP), **siege = glass cannon** (≈6× infantry damage, lowest DEF and HP), cavalry strong ATK / weak DEF, archers in between. Cycle: infantry > cavalry > archers > siege > infantry.

## Unlocking tiers

Higher troop tiers are unlocked by upgrading the matching training building: the [Barracks](../../buildings/barracks/) for infantry, the Archery Range for archers, the Stable for cavalry and the Siege Workshop for siege. Every level of a training building also trains its own troop type 1% faster, up to 30% at level 30.

:::note
These stats are starting values and may change after more playtesting.
:::
