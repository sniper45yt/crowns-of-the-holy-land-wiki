Troops you lose in a battle are either **dead** or **wounded**. Wounded troops are stored in your Hospital and can be healed. Dead troops are gone.

## The Hospital

- Each Hospital level holds **200 wounded troops** (one space per troop).
- Troops waiting in the heal queue still take up space.
- Each level also makes healing **2% faster**.
- **No Hospital means nothing fits, so every casualty dies.**
- When the Hospital cannot take everyone, the **higher tiers are saved first** and the rest die.
- Wounded troops add no Power and cost no upkeep.

## Who gets wounded

| Who | Share of losses that become wounded |
| --- | --- |
| Castle owner defending their castle | All, until the Hospital is full |
| Reinforcer defending an ally's castle | All, into their **own** Hospital |
| Attacker in a player-vs-player attack | 20%, the rest die |
| Each participant in a rally attack | 20%, into their own Hospital, the rest die |
| Player raiding an NPC camp | All, until the Hospital is full |
| NPC camp troops | None, they just die |

So **defending is much kinder to your army than attacking**, and a big Hospital is worth building before you start attacking other players.

## Example

A castle owner with a level 2 Hospital (capacity 400) already has 150 wounded, so 250 spaces are free. After a battle that costs them 700 troops, the first 250 (highest tier first) are stored as wounded and 450 die. An ally who reinforced with a level 3 Hospital (600 free spaces) and lost 500 troops stores all 500. An ally with no Hospital loses all 83 troops that fell.

## Who takes the losses

Losses depend on the **troop type**, not on whose troops they are. If the front line is infantry belonging to the owner and an ally, those die first, while the owner's archers behind them may not be touched at all. A guildmate's reinforcement standing in front can therefore protect what is behind it.

## Kills

A **kill** counts every enemy casualty, dead or wounded, including reinforcers' troops. Kills feed the rankings. In a rally, everything the defending side lost is split between the participants in proportion to the damage their troops dealt. Raids on NPC camps do not count as kills.
