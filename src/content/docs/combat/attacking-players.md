You can attack another player's castle from the world map. Combat is resolved on the server when your army arrives.

## Requirements

To attack a castle you need:

- A target that is **not shielded**.
- A **free march slot**.
- At least one troop, and no more than your **march capacity**. Each Drill Grounds level adds 200 troops.

The troops you send leave your garrison and march to the target.

## Travel time

Travel time depends only on distance: **7.5 seconds per tile**, with a minimum of 10 seconds. The type of march does not matter. The army's trip home takes the same time again. A launched rally travels at half speed (15 seconds per tile).

## The defender's army

The defending side is the castle owner's garrison **plus every reinforcement stationed there**. They fight as one army. See [Rallies and reinforcements](../rallies-and-reinforcements/).

## If you win: loot

Loot comes out of the defender's real resources. It is never invented.

- The most you can take is your army's **carrying load** (troops sent × load per troop), spread across the four resources with some random variation.
- Any resource the defender holds **below their Warehouse protection threshold cannot be looted**.
- You steal at most what the defender actually has above that threshold.

## If you lose: the defender's shield

After a **successful defence**, the defender gets a **4 hour shield**, during which they cannot be attacked.

:::note
Shields are still being redesigned, so this may change.
:::

## Recalling an army

Before your army arrives you can cancel the attack with a **March Recall** item. The troops return home and no battle takes place.

## Reports

When the battle ends, everyone involved gets the same report at the same moment: the attacker, the castle owner and every reinforcer, even one whose troops were untouched. Reports show kills, how many troops survived, were wounded or died on each side, and a breakdown by troop type.

## Scouting

The **Lighthouse** is required to send scouts. Higher levels reveal more in a scout report: which troop tiers you can identify, how exact the troop counts are and, at high levels, the reinforcements stationed in the castle. Scouts travel three times faster than an ordinary march.
