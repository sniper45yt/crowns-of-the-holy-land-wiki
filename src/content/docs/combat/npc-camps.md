Camps on the world map are held by non-player troops. They range from **level 1 to level 30**. Fights use exactly the same combat rules as against players, with no special treatment for either side.

## Garrison

A camp's garrison is infantry of the tier unlocked at its level, plus archers equal to 40% of the infantry. The infantry count is about `22 × 1.173^(level−1)`.

## How strong a march you need

Infantry of the camp's own tier needed to win (no random roll):

| Camp level | Troops needed |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 40 |
| 5 | 75 |
| 10 | 150 |
| 16 | 385 |
| 30 | 3,500 |

The biggest march a player can send is 6,000 troops (Drill Grounds level 30 gives 200 per level, plus 50 per Leadership research level). A **level 30 camp** is beaten by a full march of tier 8 or higher infantry, or a tier 6 or higher infantry and cavalry mix. It cannot be beaten by tier 7 or lower infantry, however many you send. See [the walkthroughs](../walkthroughs/) for the full numbers.

## Rewards

- **Winning removes the camp** from the map for everyone.
- You gain **Lord XP**.
- Loot is the camp's loot × (0.5 + 0.5 × the share of your health left) × your loot research. Winning with more of your army intact means more loot.
- A loss leaves the camp with its full garrison.

## Your losses

Everything you lose becomes **wounded** until your Hospital is full, so a big Hospital makes camp raiding almost free of permanent losses. Camp troops simply die.
